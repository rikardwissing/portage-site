Download

Originals, transcodes, or both

Grab the file as it sits on the server, or let the server shrink it first. Whole seasons queue in one go.

Convert

Convert for the device you own

Some TVs and browsers can't decode every format. Portage converts on the device, with hardware encoding, and every conversion checkpoints as it goes.

  • Presets for Apple TV, Chromecast and iPhone pick the right codec and container for you
  • Keep every audio track, pick a subtitle to carry along or burn in
  • Pause, quit, restart — a conversion resumes where it stopped, frame exact
Cast

Cast to any screen

Chromecast, Google TV and DLNA renderers on your network. AirPlay. And any web browser: open the receiver page on the screen you want, enter the code, done.

  • Switch audio tracks on the TV without restarting the film
  • Stay in control from your phone or Mac: seek, pause, pick subtitles
  • Casting a file the TV can't play? Portage offers to convert it first
Library

Downloads and conversions live in one list with their codec, resolution and size in plain sight. Play, cast or convert again from the same row.

  • Three server types and local folders, side by side in one sidebar
  • Continue Watching, search across every source, Spotlight on your library
  • Local folders play without any server at all
