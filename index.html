Questions

Do I need a media server?

Portage is a client for a Plex, Emby or Jellyfin server you already have. Without one, you can point it at a folder of video files and use that as a library.

Do I have to download before I can watch?

No. Anything on your server streams straight away, at full quality as the original file whenever your device can play it. Download when you want it with you offline.

How do I cast to a browser?

Open tv.rikard.dev in any browser: a laptop, a smart TV, a car's screen. It shows a code. Pick Web browser in Portage's cast menu and type the code, or scan it on iPhone. It works on your own network and away from it.

Does it work away from home?

If your server is reachable from outside your network, yes, the same way the official apps reach it. Anything you've downloaded plays without any connection.

Do I need a Plex Pass to download?

No. Portage downloads with the access your server already gives your account.

Is Portage affiliated with Plex, Emby or Jellyfin?

No. It's an independent app that talks to their servers. It has no account of its own and hosts no content.
