Features

  • Cast to any TV

    Send a film to a Chromecast, a smart TV or AirPlay, and use your phone as the remote.

  • Any browser

    Open tv.rikard.dev on a hotel TV, a laptop or a car’s screen, type the code, and it plays.

  • Made for your TV

    When a TV can’t play a file, your iPhone, iPad or Mac makes a copy it can. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Offline

    Download a film or a whole season, pause any time, and watch it with no connection.

  • Original quality

    Portage plays the file your server has whenever it can, so your server converts nothing.

  • Every track

    Switch audio and subtitles while it plays, from the menu you already know.

  • Surround sound

    Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus play untouched, with Dolby Atmos passed through to a compatible receiver. DTS and TrueHD keep 5.1 when Portage converts them on your device.

  • Every server

    Plex, Emby and Jellyfin side by side, plus folders of files, in one library.

  • Carry on anywhere

    Where you stop is saved on your server, so you can pick up on any device.

  • Picture in picture

    Keep a film playing in a corner while you use other apps.

  • Spotlight search

    Find the films and episodes you’ve downloaded straight from Spotlight.

Questions

Do I need a media server?

Portage plays from a Plex, Emby or Jellyfin server you already have. Without one, point it at a folder of video files.

Which TVs does it work with?

Chromecast and Google TV, smart TVs on your Wi-Fi, Apple TV with AirPlay, and any screen with a web browser.

How do I cast to a browser?

Open tv.rikard.dev in any browser: a laptop, a smart TV, a car’s screen. Pick Web browser in Portage and type the code it shows, or scan it.

Does it work away from home?

If your server is reachable from outside, yes, the same way the official apps reach it. Downloads play with no connection at all.

Is Portage made by Plex, Emby or Jellyfin?

No. It works with their servers, but they don’t make or endorse it.
