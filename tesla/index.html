How it works

Four steps, about a minute.

Nothing to install in the car: the page at tv.rikard.dev is the receiver, and the Tesla's own browser opens it.

  1. Park and open the browser

    Shift into Park, open Browser from the app launcher and go to tv.rikard.dev. It shows Ready to cast and a six-character code. Bookmark it for next time.

  2. Play something in Portage

    On your iPhone, iPad or Mac, start a film or an episode from your server or your downloads. Open the cast menu and pick Web browser…

  3. Enter the code

    Type the code from the touchscreen. On iPhone you can tap Scan the browser's code and scan the QR code the page shows instead.

  4. Watch on the touchscreen

    It plays on the Tesla's screen. Pause, seek and switch audio or subtitles from your phone.

Before you start

What your Tesla needs.

Tesla's browser is built on Chromium, the same engine as Chrome. These are the car's own rules.

Park

Tesla's browser plays video only in Park. Shift out of Park and it pauses.

A connection

On Wi-Fi, at home or on your phone's hotspot, the browser works on any plan. Over the car's own LTE it needs Premium Connectivity.

Any Tesla since 2018

Every Model 3, Model Y and Cybertruck, and Model S and X built from March 2018. An older S or X needs the MCU2 upgrade, because the original computer's browser doesn't play video.

Where it comes from

Your server, or your phone.

If the Tesla's browser can't play a file, Portage says so and can convert it on your device into one that will.

From your server

A film on your Plex, Emby or Jellyfin server goes from the server to the Tesla. The server has to be reachable from outside your home, the same as when you watch on your phone away from home.

From your downloads

A film you've downloaded goes from your phone to the Tesla directly, so a Supercharger stop needs nothing from home.

Not through us

tv.rikard.dev only introduces your phone to the car's browser. After that they talk directly, and no video passes through it.

Next time

Remembered, no code.

Once paired, the Tesla is a cast target of its own. Open the bookmark in the car and it's in Portage's cast menu by name.

Paired browsers

Paired browsers receive casts without a code while their page is open.

Name it

Call it Tesla in Settings, under Paired browsers, so it's easy to find in the cast menu.

Other cars

Polestar, Renault and some Mercedes-Benz and Audi models work the same way through Vivaldi. Cast to your car has the rest.

Questions

Can I watch while driving?

No. Tesla's browser plays video only in Park and pauses it when you shift out of Park. It's for charging stops and waiting.

Do I need Premium Connectivity?

Only to use the browser over the car's own LTE. On Wi-Fi, at home or on your phone's hotspot, you don't.

Does the video fill the screen?

Recent Tesla software has a full-screen button in the browser. Tap it to give the video the whole touchscreen.

Is there a Portage app for the Tesla?

No, and none is needed. The Tesla's own browser opens tv.rikard.dev, and Portage on your iPhone, iPad or Mac does the rest. Portage is not made by or affiliated with Tesla.
