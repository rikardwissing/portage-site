Press

Portage is a player for Plex, Emby and Jellyfin on iPhone, iPad and Mac. It casts to any TV, and converts files for TVs that can’t play them.

The story

We were stuck in an Airbnb with an old Apple TV and couldn’t stream from Plex to it. Portage is the app that could: your own films, from your own server, on whatever screen is in front of you, whether that’s an old Apple TV, a Chromecast, a smart TV, a hotel TV or a car’s browser.
