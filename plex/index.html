Questions

Do I need a Plex Pass to download?

No. Portage downloads with the access your server already gives your account.

Where do Plex downloads go?

On iPhone and iPad, the Files app under On My iPhone → Portage. On the Mac, the folder you pick the first time you download. They're ordinary video files that open in any player.

Is Portage the official Plex app?

No. Portage is an independent app, not made by or affiliated with Plex. It signs in with your Plex account and connects to your servers like other Plex clients do.

What do I need?

A Plex account with access to a server, and an iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or a Mac on macOS 14 or later. Portage is free, with no account of its own and no ads.
